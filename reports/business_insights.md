\# Superstore Sales Analytics — Business Insights



\## 1. Project Overview



An end-to-end sales analytics project using the Sample Superstore dataset. The objective is to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product trends, regional performance, and discount effectiveness using Python, SQL, and data visualization.



\## 2. Dataset Overview



\- Total records: 9,994

\- Total columns: 21

\- Unique customers: 793

\- Unique products: 1,862

\- Total orders: 5,009

\- Missing values: 11 (all in postal\_code)



All 9,994 records were retained after data cleaning.



\## 3. Overall Business Performance



| KPI | Result |

|---|---:|

| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |

| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |

| Profit Margin | 12.47% |

| Total Orders | 5,009 |

| Average Order Value | $458.61 |

| Units Sold | 37,873 |



\## 4. Category Performance



\- Technology generated the highest sales at approximately $836,154 and the highest profit at approximately $145,455.

\- Office Supplies generated approximately $122,491 in profit, with a margin of 17.04%.

\- Furniture generated approximately $742,000 in sales but only approximately $18,451 in profit, resulting in a low margin of 2.49%.



\*\*Recommendation:\*\* Investigate Furniture pricing, discounting, and product-level costs to identify opportunities to improve profitability.



\## 5. Regional Performance



\- West led sales and profit, generating approximately $725,458 in sales and $108,418 in profit.

\- East ranked second, with approximately $678,781 in sales and $91,523 in profit.

\- Central generated approximately $501,240 in sales but only $39,706 in profit.

\- South generated approximately $391,722 in sales and $46,749 in profit.



\*\*Recommendation:\*\* Investigate the profitability gap in Central and identify successful practices from West and East that could be replicated.



\## 6. Product-Level Insights



\- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-selling product, generating approximately $61,600 in sales.

\- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print recorded the largest loss among the ten lowest-profit products, at approximately -$8,880.



\*\*Recommendation:\*\* Review pricing, discounting, and cost structures for consistently loss-making products.



\## 7. Discount Analysis



\- Transactions with discounts of 30% and above showed negative aggregate profit in this dataset.

\- The 70% discount group recorded approximately $40,075 in losses.

\- The 80% discount group recorded approximately $30,539 in losses.



\*\*Recommendation:\*\* Review high-discount transactions and establish discount thresholds based on product-level profitability.



\*Note: These are descriptive associations. They do not establish that discounts alone caused losses.\*
